Jakobys Smash Burgers

Take out your bowl and all the seasonings.

Step 1: Take one pound of ground beef and place it in the bowl.

Step 2: Add salt, pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, and smoked peprika into the bowl and thoroughly mix it into the beef

Step 3: Evenly divide the beef into a even amount of ball, you need 2 patties per sandwitch.

Step 4: Heat a skillet over medium-high heat and add a little oil.

Step 5: Place 2 patties in the skillet at a time and cook for 3-4 minutes on each side until fully cooked. after 2 minuets place the cheese on top of the burgers and place a cover over the pan allowing them to melt

Step 6: While the patties are cooking, prepare your buns by buttering them lightly and toasting them.

Step 7: Assemble the burgers by adding 2 cooked stacked on top of eachother onto the bun and any other toppings you would like.

Step 8: serve on a plate with fries and a sauce of your choosing.
